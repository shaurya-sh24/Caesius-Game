# Caesius respository

Harpoon can be a big help in attacking and moving under the deep pressure!

Take bubbles after attacking the animals to complete your oxygen level

Health is represented by the face on the upper left and oxygen level is represented by the breathing sound of the character

Controls:

WASD - move

Left Click - Fire harpoon

Left Click - Retrieve harpoon

Right Click - Pull player to attached harpoon
